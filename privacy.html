Privacy Policy, Cookie Disclosure & Terms of Use

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective for domain: https://3x3chess.com

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At 3x3Chess Studio (accessible from https://3x3chess.com), protecting the privacy of our visitors and players is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by 3x3Chess Studio and how we use them in compliance with international data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

3x3Chess Studio does not require users to register an account, provide a password, or submit sensitive personal data to play our chess endgame puzzles or read our educational guides.

2. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

To provide a seamless gaming experience, our web application utilizes your browser's native localStorage mechanism (under the key 3x3chess_studio_prefs_v1). This stores strictly non-personal user interface preferences directly on your device, including:

  • Selected interface language (EN, PT, ES, FR, DE, or RU);
  • Current puzzle difficulty level (Mate in 1 through Mate in 15);
  • Audio synthesizer volume, sound effects toggle, and board orientation settings.

This data never leaves your browser, is never transmitted to external servers, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

We partner with third-party advertising networks, primarily Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on 3x3chess.com based on a user's prior visits to our website and other sites on the Internet.

  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to 3x3chess.com and/or other websites on the Internet.
  • How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of the use of personalized advertising cookies at any time by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • More Information on Google Data Usage: To understand how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Google Analytics (GA4) & Log Files

3x3Chess Studio uses Google Analytics 4 to analyze aggregated, anonymous traffic patterns (such as page views, session duration, device categories, and general geographic region). Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to report on user interactions without identifying individual visitors personally. Standard server log files also record IP addresses, browser types, and timestamps solely for security monitoring and server administration.

5. GDPR & LGPD Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area and Brazil), you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any personal data you may have voluntarily submitted to us (for example, via email support inquiries), as well as the right to restrict processing or withdraw consent for advertising cookies via our consent management banner or browser settings.
